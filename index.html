Frequently Asked Questions

Are these tools really free?

Yes. All tools are free with no sign up and no limits.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Everything runs inside your browser, so your photos and PDFs never leave your device.

Will compressing an image reduce its quality?

A little, but you control the quality slider. Around 70 to 80 percent keeps the image sharp while cutting the size a lot.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The site is built for mobile phones and works in Chrome, Safari and Firefox.

Why is my PDF not converting?

Password protected PDFs cannot be opened. Remove the password first and try again.
