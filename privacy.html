Privacy Policy

Your files stay with you

This website may show ads from third parties such as Google AdSense. These partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can manage ad personalization in your Google Ads Settings.

We load open-source code libraries (PDF.js and a QR code library) from cdnjs.cloudflare.com to make the tools work.
